# Hoop Solo Practice

Terms for a player who sets a phone or tablet down and practices basketball alone.

## Language

**Setup**:
The preparation phase in which the player positions the device and checks the court view before beginning practice.
_Avoid_: Warm-up, calibration

**Practice**:
The activity in which the player shoots baskets and follows the recorded score.
_Avoid_: Setup, workout program

**Pause**:
A temporary interruption of practice that retains the player's recorded score for continuation.
_Avoid_: Reset, end practice

**Rim Lock**:
A fixed reference area marking the basket rim, chosen by the player.
_Avoid_: Camera lock, device lock
